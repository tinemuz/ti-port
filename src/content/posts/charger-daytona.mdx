The 1969 Dodge Charger Daytona is an icon of American muscle, a car that pushed the boundaries of performance and style. Inspired by the incredible work of Marcell Sebestyen, I set out to reimagine this legendary vehicle. My goal was to create a design that honors its classic, unforgettable lines while infusing it with a bold vision of the future.

This project was brought to life using Blender for the detailed modeling and KeyShot for the final renders.

I hope you enjoy this modern take on a timeless classic!
